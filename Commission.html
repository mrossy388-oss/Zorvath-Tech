Commission Policy

Effective Date: 20 July 2026

This Commission Policy explains the product-based business model, commission calculation, level advancement, withdrawal eligibility, and other applicable rules of Zorvath Tech Private Limited.

All commissions and benefits are subject to eligible product transactions, applicable company policies, verification requirements, and the terms applicable to the platform.

1. Product-Based Business Model

Zorvath Tech follows a product-based direct selling business model. Users and registered partners can purchase products and participate in eligible product-related business activities through the platform.

Commissions and benefits are generated only through eligible product purchases and sales activities. No commission is provided solely for joining, registering, or adding members.

2. Commission Calculation Policy

Applicable commissions are calculated based on eligible profit generated from product transactions and distributed according to the applicable level-wise commission structure.

Commission eligibility depends on:

  • Eligible product purchases
  • Product sales activities
  • Applicable level criteria
  • Company policies and terms

Commission amounts may vary based on eligible transactions and applicable business rules.

Profit-Based Reward Allocation

For each eligible product purchase, 2% of the eligible profit generated from that transaction is allocated towards the applicable reward/commission pool.

The 2% allocation is calculated on the profit amount and not on the total product selling price or purchase value.

Rewards and commissions are distributed to eligible partners according to the applicable level-wise commission structure and company policies.

3. Level Advancement Criteria

Zorvath Tech follows a level-based commission structure.

Level advancement is determined based on eligible product purchase and sales criteria defined by the company.

Initial Purchase & Level Eligibility

Partners who make an initial first product purchase of ₹15,000 or more are eligible for the applicable Level 7 commission structure, subject to the Company's policies and compensation plan.

If the initial first product purchase is less than ₹15,000 (for example, ₹500), the partner will not become directly eligible for Level 7 based on any subsequent purchase amount. In such cases, level advancement will be determined only in accordance with the Company's standard level progression criteria and applicable business policies.

Level Advancement Criteria Modification

Zorvath Tech Private Limited reserves the right to modify, revise, increase, or decrease the required sales volume criteria, qualification requirements, or advancement conditions for achieving any level in the compensation plan from time to time based on business requirements and company policies.

Any changes will be communicated through official channels.

5. Withdrawal Eligibility

Commission withdrawal is subject to:

  • Successful completion of eligible transactions
  • KYC verification
  • Applicable withdrawal rules
  • Company verification process

The company may verify transactions before processing withdrawals.

6. Fraud Prevention

Zorvath Tech reserves the right to suspend, adjust, or cancel commissions generated through:

  • Fake transactions
  • Fraudulent activities
  • Misuse of the platform
  • Violation of company policies

7. Policy Updates

Zorvath Tech reserves the right to modify, update, or revise commission structures, level advancement criteria, eligibility requirements, and business policies based on business requirements.

Any changes will be communicated through updated policies or appropriate communication channels.

8. Taxes and Commission Deductions

Commission earnings are subject to applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.

Where required by law, applicable taxes or statutory deductions, including Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), if applicable, may be deducted from commission payouts before processing withdrawals.

Members are responsible for complying with their individual tax obligations.

The Company reserves the right to modify tax deduction procedures in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

9. Important Notice

Zorvath Tech operates as a product-focused digital commerce platform. Partner benefits and commissions are linked to eligible product purchases and sales activities as per applicable company policies and terms.

Participation in the business opportunity does not guarantee any fixed income or profit.

Earnings depend on eligible product purchases, sales performance, commission eligibility, and compliance with company policies.
